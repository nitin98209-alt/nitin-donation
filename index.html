My Story

My name is Nitin Sharma, and I’m an individual in India trying to build a path toward financial independence.

My family is currently going through financial difficulties. My parents already have pending loans and other financial responsibilities, so they are not in a position to afford a laptop for me, and I don't want to put any additional burden on them. I also cannot take a loan myself, and unfortunately, my relatives are not able to help me with the cost either.

I’ve tried to find opportunities to earn money, including freelancing and work-from-home and work-from-office jobs. I have applied for different opportunities, and in so many cases, my skills and resume were suitable. I even cleared assessments and interviews, but not having the necessary equipment became a major obstacle.

Right now, a reliable laptop is one of the biggest things I need. With a laptop, I would be able to start freelancing, apply for jobs, continue my studies, develop my skills, and work toward becoming financially independent. More importantly, I want to eventually support my education and help my family with our financial situation.

My goal is to raise ₹23,000 for a basic laptop that I can use for work, freelancing, learning, studying, and building my career. My goal isn't simply to buy a device—it is to have a practical tool that can give me an opportunity to start earning.

If you are able and genuinely willing to help, even ₹50 or ₹200 would mean a lot to me. ₹50–₹200 may seem like a small amount to many people, but when many people contribute a small amount, it can make a very big difference for someone in my situation.

I'm not asking anyone to give more than they can afford. If you genuinely want to support me, any voluntary contribution would bring me one step closer to getting the laptop I need to start working.

Thank you for taking the time to read my story and for considering helping me. Even a small contribution could help me take an important step toward becoming financially independent.
